Get involved

Noctillio AI runs on contributors

There's no paid team behind this — just people who use these tools and want them to be better. Here's how to jump in.

Ship code

Pick up an open issue on AutoTimm or NightFlow, or fix something that's bugging you. PRs of any size — a new backbone wrapper, a bug fix, a perf tweak — are welcome.

Propose ideas

Have a feature in mind, or an idea for a whole new tool — a video-processing pipeline, a VLM fine-tuning helper? Open a discussion or issue and let's shape it together.

Improve docs & tutorials

Docs age faster than code. Clarifying a setup step, adding an example notebook, or writing a walkthrough is as valuable as a feature PR.

Test on your setup

Different GPUs, OSes, and datasets surface different bugs. Running AutoTimm or NightFlow on your own hardware and reporting what breaks is genuinely useful contribution.

How a contribution usually goes

01

Pick a project

Start with AutoTimm or NightFlow, or bring a new idea to the org.

02

Find or file an issue

Browse open issues for a good first contribution, or open one describing what you'd like to work on.

03

Fork, build, and open a PR

Fork the repo, make your change, and open a pull request — small, focused PRs get reviewed fastest.

04

Iterate with the maintainers

Expect review feedback, not silence. We'll work through it with you until it's ready to merge.
